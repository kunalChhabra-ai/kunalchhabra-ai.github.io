Private Limited Company Registration in Lucknow

Looking to register a Private Limited Company in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh? CA Kunal Chhabra provides 100% online Pvt Ltd registration services for entrepreneurs and businesses across Lucknow — fast turnaround, transparent pricing, and complete CA support.

Why Register a Pvt Ltd in Lucknow?

  • Limited Liability: Personal assets of directors protected
  • Separate Legal Entity: Company can own property, enter contracts
  • Funding Ready: VCs and angel investors prefer Pvt Ltd structure
  • Tax Benefits: Corporate tax at 22–25% vs higher individual rates
  • Credibility: Clients in Lucknow trust Pvt Ltd companies more

Requirements

  • Minimum 2 Directors (maximum 15)
  • Minimum 2 Shareholders (maximum 200)
  • At least one Indian Resident Director
  • Registered office address in Lucknow or anywhere in India

Documents Required

  • PAN Card of all directors
  • Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Bank statement / utility bill (not older than 2 months)
  • Electricity bill + NOC for registered office address

Registration Process

  1. Obtain DSC — Digital Signature Certificate (1–2 days)
  2. Name Reservation via RUN on MCA portal (1–3 days)
  3. Draft MOA & AOA
  4. File SPICe+ form — incorporation, PAN, TAN, GST (3–7 days)
  5. Receive Certificate of Incorporation with CIN
