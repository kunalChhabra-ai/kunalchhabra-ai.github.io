---- title: "Understanding GST on Director’s Services: Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) Explained" date: "2025-05-15" description: "Confused about GST applicability on director's remuneration in India? Explore the rules under Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) for corporate compliance." --- Navigating Indian taxation and corporate compliance can often feel like walking through a labyrinth. One of the most common yet misunderstood queries we receive from startup founders and directors at our Chartered Accountant firm is: **"Is GST applicable on the remuneration paid to company directors?"** The short answer is yes, but the mechanics of *who* pays the tax and *how* it is paid depend entirely on the nature of the services provided and the capacity in which the director acts. In this blog post, we break down the GST implications on director’s remuneration under the Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) regime, specifically focusing on the Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM). --- ### The Core Principle: RCM on Director's Services Under Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax (Rate), the Indian government mandated that services supplied by a director of a company or a body corporate to the said company or body corporate shall be taxed under the **Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)**. What does this mean for your business? * **Standard GST:** Normally, the supplier of services collects GST from the recipient and deposits it with the government. * **Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM):** Under RCM, the recipient of the services (in this case, the company) is directly liable to pay the GST to the government, rather than the supplier (the director). Therefore, if your company pays a fee, sitting fees, or remuneration to a director, the company must self-assess and pay the applicable GST directly to the exchequer. --- ### Key Distinction: Employee vs. Independent Director To determine the GST liability accurately, we must classify the director’s role within the company: #### 1. Executive / Whole-time Directors (Employees) If a director is also an employee of the company (e.g., a Managing Director or Whole-time Director drawing a salary reflected under TDS Section 192), such services fall under **Schedule III of the CGST Act, 2017**. * **The Rule:** Services by an employee to the employer in the course of or in relation to their employment are **not treated as supply of goods or services**. * **GST Implication:** **No GST is applicable** on the regular salary paid to executive directors where an employer-employee relationship exists, and TDS is deducted under salary heads. #### 2. Independent / Non-Executive Directors Non-executive directors or independent directors do not have an employment contract with the company. The remuneration paid to them (such as sitting fees or professional retainers) is typically subjected to TDS under Section 194J (Professional Services). * **The Rule:** Because there is no employer-employee relationship, their services are considered a taxable supply. * **GST Implication:** **GST at 18% applies** and must be paid by the company under RCM. --- ### Compliance Checklist for Companies To ensure seamless corporate compliance and avoid interest and penalties during a GST audit, companies must follow this operational checklist: 1. **Self-Invoice Generation:** Under Section 31(3)(f) of the CGST Act, the company must issue a self-invoice for the services received from the director on the date of payment. 2. **Monthly RCM Payment:** Pay the 18% GST through the Electronic Cash Ledger under RCM in the monthly GSTR-3B filing. 3. **Input Tax Credit (ITC):** The company can generally claim ITC on the GST paid under RCM, provided the expenses are used in the course or furtherance of business and are not blocked under Section 17(5). --- ### Final Thoughts for Business Owners Misinterpreting GST rules on director remuneration can lead to avoidable tax liabilities, interest charges, and compliance friction during GST assessments. Whether you operate in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, or anywhere across India, ensuring your finance team correctly segregates executive salaries from independent director fees is critical. *Disclaimer: Tax laws are subject to frequent updates through notifications and rulings by the Authority for Advance Rulings (AAR).
