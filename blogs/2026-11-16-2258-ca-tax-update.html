Understanding FEMA and FDI Frameworks for Indian Startups

When international institutional funds, venture capitalists, or angel investors infuse capital into an Indian Private Limited company, the transaction falls directly under the regulatory purview of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) via the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999.
